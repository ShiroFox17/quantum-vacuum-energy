# Project: Quantum Vacuum Energy & Spacetime Translocation

This project explores the non-equilibrium extraction of zero-point energy using an 8-site cyclic quantum meta-material.

- **Negative Energy Manifold**: Realizing a stable steady state for energy harvesting and potential spacetime curvature control (Anti-gravity/Translocation).
- **Inspiration**: Ancient mathematical constants integrated with modern Quantum Field Theory.
